### Naming Convention & Philosophy

The project’s nomenclature is derived from industrial infrastructure, reflecting the relationship between fluid logic and structural stability.

**Chute (The Language)**
The name **Chute** represents the language’s core design principle: **linear, gravity-fed execution.** Just as a chute facilitates the effortless, one-way movement of material, the language is optimized for top-to-bottom token generation and pipeline-driven logic. It avoids the "pyramids of doom" found in nested architectures, offering a flowy developer experience where data drops through transformations toward a final result. By banning unbounded loops, execution is guaranteed to move in only one direction—down.

**Ballast (The Runtime)**
While the language is light and fluid, the environment that executes it must be heavy and immovable. **Ballast** refers to the dense material used to provide stability to ships and railway tracks. As a runtime, Ballast serves as the "grounding" force for the LLM. It provides the essential weight of safety—handling sandboxing, resource limits, and capability verification—ensuring that the script's execution remains stable and predictable regardless of the complexity of the task.

**The Synergy**
Together, they form a complete system: **Chute** provides the guided path for the agent’s intent, while **Ballast** provides the structural foundation that keeps the system upright.
